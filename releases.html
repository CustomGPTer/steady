Release notes

What changed in each version of Steady. The newest is at the top.

Version 1.0.0 22 November 2026Latest

  • First release. Setup for alcohol, drugs or both, with a goal for each (stop, cut down or not sure yet), the day you last used, Stopping safely and a short AUDIT or Severity of Dependence Scale if you want it.
  • Home: a dawn sky that brightens as clear days add up, your current run and total clear days (the total never resets), this week's dots, and cards for whatever needs you next.
  • Check-ins: urge from 0 to 10, whether you drank or used, drinks in UK units, feelings, HALT and triggers, place, company, what helped and a note. Safety cards for emergencies, crisis and withdrawal.
  • Urge now: ride the urge, wait fifteen minutes, play it forward, your reasons, reach out to up to five people, breathing, grounding and UK help lines.
  • A kind lapse review that leads to a plan for next time.
  • Thought Reframe with eleven permission-giving thoughts, Weigh it up, Risky times plans with private reminders, and Rebuild ladders.
  • Learn: 40 short lessons in eight topics, ten five-day programmes suggested from your plan, and your reflections.
  • Insights: clear days, urges over time, time of day and weekday maps, what seems to lead to use, the tools that help you most, sleep and your body, and skills practised, each pattern marked as an early sign, an emerging pattern or a clear pattern.
  • The AUDIT at setup and the AUDIT-C each month for alcohol, and the Severity of Dependence Scale for each drug, with scores over time.
  • Gains: money and time back from clear days, gentle notes on what often changes, and milestones with a picture to share.
  • A drink diary for cutting down, with UK units and a weekly limit you set (14 by default, the UK Chief Medical Officers' guideline).
  • Routines: meetings, appointments and prescribed medicines to tick off, with optional reminders.
  • Monthly and yearly letters, a PDF report for your GP or keyworker, and spreadsheets of every part of your data.
  • Widgets: a one-tap check-in, this week's sky, and Urge now, which opens even with the app lock on.
  • Reminders for a check-in and today's programme step: never more than two a day, never in quiet hours, worded so they stay private.
  • Health Connect: sleep, steps, resting heart rate and heart rate variability beside your urges, and calm sessions saved as mindfulness.
  • Backup: a password-locked backup file, a daily copy to a folder you choose keeping the newest seven, and restore with undo.
  • App lock with a PIN or fingerprint, a choice of six home-screen names and eight icons, light and dark themes, five accent colours and reduced motion.

Checking your download

The file for version 1.0.0 is called Steady.apk. It is 3.1 MB (3,275,512 bytes). Its SHA-256 checksum is:

19b162e9f7fa1b2be8ea7d601f5bf5082d09ecf99d55406554cb20e1f6a5b148

On Windows, run certutil -hashfile Steady.apk SHA256 and compare. Every version is signed with the same key, so each one installs over the last and keeps what you have saved.

Older versions are on the GitHub releases page.
