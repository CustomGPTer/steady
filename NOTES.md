Steady 1.0.0, the first release.

Steady is a free Android app for getting sober, coming off drugs, or both. It has no internet permission, no account, no adverts and no tracking. Everything is encrypted on your phone.

- Setup for alcohol, drugs or both, with a goal for each: stop, cut down or not sure yet. Stopping safely comes first.
- Clear days: your current run and a total that never resets after a lapse, on a dawn sky that brightens as they add up.
- Check-ins in about thirty seconds, with drinks counted in UK units, and safety cards for emergencies, crisis and withdrawal.
- Urge now: ride it out, wait fifteen minutes, play it forward, your reasons, your people, breathing, grounding and UK help lines.
- A kind lapse review, Thought Reframe, Weigh it up, Risky times plans with private reminders, and Rebuild ladders.
- 40 short lessons in eight topics and ten five-day programmes.
- Insights, gains, milestones, a drink diary with a weekly limit, routines, and monthly letters.
- The AUDIT and AUDIT-C for alcohol and the Severity of Dependence Scale for drugs, with scores over time.
- A PDF report for your GP or keyworker, spreadsheets, password-locked backups, widgets, Health Connect, an app lock and a discreet name and icon.

Android 8 or later. Website: https://customgpter.github.io/steady/
