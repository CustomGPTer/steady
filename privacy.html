Privacy policy

Last updated 22 November 2026. This policy covers the Steady app for Android (package uk.jj.steady) and this website. Steady is made by Jonathan Jackson, an individual developer in the United Kingdom.

The short version

  • Everything you put into Steady is kept on your phone, encrypted. It is never uploaded, to the developer or to anyone else.
  • Steady does not hold Android's internet permission. It cannot go online, so it cannot send anything anywhere.
  • There are no accounts, no servers, no advertising and no analytics. The developer receives nothing from your phone.
  • Copies leave the app only when you make them: a backup, a report, a spreadsheet or a shared picture.
  • Health Connect is optional. Health data is read on your phone, shown to you and never stored in backups or exports.

What stays on your phone

Steady keeps everything in one file in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. The file is encrypted with AES-256 using a key held by Android's Keystore, so a copy taken off the phone cannot be read.

  • Your plan: what you want to change, your goal for each, the day you last used and any weekly cost and time you entered.
  • Your check-ins: the urge level, the time, whether you drank or used, any drinks and amounts, and any feelings, triggers, place, company, what helped and note you added.
  • Lapse reviews, Urge now and breathing sessions, thought records, Weigh it up lists, risky-time plans, Rebuild ladders and your routines.
  • Lessons read, programme steps done and the reflections you wrote.
  • Your AUDIT, AUDIT-C and Severity of Dependence Scale answers and scores.
  • Your reasons and the names and numbers of up to five support people, which you type in yourself. Steady does not read your contacts.
  • Your settings, including reminder times, the app's name and icon, and your app lock. The PIN itself is not stored: only a salted, one-way hash of it.
  • If you set a backup password: a key made from it, kept inside the encrypted file, so daily backups can be made without asking you each time.

Health Connect

If you turn Health Connect on, Steady reads the types you allow, for the period you are looking at, and uses them on your phone to compare with your check-ins. It does not keep a copy: the numbers are read again each time. They are not included in backups, spreadsheets or the monthly letters. They appear in a report only if you make one and choose to share it.

If you allow it, breathing, grounding and riding-the-urge sessions of a minute or more are saved to Health Connect as mindfulness sessions, with their title and times. You can remove the access at any time in Health Connect, and the data Steady saved stays in Health Connect until you delete it there.

Notes and sensitive information

What you record about drinking and drug use is health information, and it is treated as such. It never leaves your phone unless you save or share a copy yourself.

When you type a note, Steady checks it on your phone for words that suggest an emergency, thoughts of suicide or self-harm, or withdrawal that may need medical help, so it can show a card with the right help. The check happens entirely on the phone. Nothing is sent, logged or remembered.

Backups, reports and exports

These are made only when you ask, and go only where you choose:

  • Backup file. Everything except your PIN and the backup settings, compressed and encrypted with AES-256 using a key made from your backup password. Without the password it cannot be opened, by anyone.
  • Daily backup. The same file, saved to the one folder you picked, once a day when something has changed. The newest seven are kept and older ones deleted. If that folder is synced to a cloud service, that service's own privacy terms apply to the copy.
  • Report. A PDF of the period you choose. Notes are left out unless you switch them on.
  • Spreadsheets. CSV files of your check-ins, drinks and other records. These are not encrypted, so keep them somewhere safe.
  • Share pictures. A milestone or a monthly picture showing numbers and colours only, never your notes and never what you drank or used.

When you share a file, Android's share sheet passes it to the app you pick, and that app's own privacy terms apply.

Widgets and notifications

The sky widget shows your clear-day number and the colours of this week, under the name you gave the app. With the app lock on, it shows only the sky. The check-in and Urge now widgets show no data at all. Notifications are marked private, so your lock screen shows only the app's name, and their wording never mentions drink or drugs.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays until you delete it. You can delete any single check-in, review, session, plan, routine or questionnaire result, or everything at once in Settings, under Your data. Uninstalling Steady deletes everything the app holds. Backup files, reports and spreadsheets you saved elsewhere are yours to delete.

Your rights

Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to request, correct or erase on a server. Everything is already in your hands: you can see it in the app, export it in full and delete it at any time.

Security

The data file is encrypted with a key that never leaves Android's Keystore. The app lock uses a PIN of four to six digits, stored only as a PBKDF2 hash, and pauses after five wrong tries. Android's own app backup is switched off for Steady, so your entries are not copied to a Google account without your knowledge. No method is perfect: keep your phone locked and your backup password private.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no scripts. GitHub may keep standard web server logs, such as IP addresses, for security, under GitHub's privacy statement. Downloads come from GitHub's servers on the same terms.

Children

Steady is made for adults. It collects nothing from anyone, but its lessons and tools are written for adults. Young people worried about drink or drugs can talk to FRANK on 0300 123 6600, a trusted adult or their GP.
