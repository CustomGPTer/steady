What Steady is

Steady is a self-help tool for people who want to stop or cut down on alcohol or drugs. It uses methods from cognitive behavioural therapy, motivational interviewing and relapse prevention to help you notice patterns, get through cravings and practise skills. It is not a medical device, a diagnosis, a treatment, a detox programme or a crisis service, and it does not give medical advice.

The lessons, programmes and tools are general. They are not tailored to your health, your history or any medication you take. The harm-reduction information is general too, and no way of using drink or drugs is safe.

Your health

Stopping alcohol or benzodiazepines suddenly after heavy or regular use can cause dangerous withdrawal, including seizures, and can be life-threatening. Talk to your GP or a local drug and alcohol service before you stop or cut down a lot. Do not use Steady in place of medical care or against medical advice, and do not stop or change any prescribed medication because of anything in the app.

Some exercises, such as breath holds, can feel uncomfortable. Go at your own pace and stop if you feel faint, dizzy or unwell.

In a crisis

Steady cannot respond to an emergency and nobody monitors what you write. If you or someone else is in danger, call 999. Samaritans are free on 116 123, any time. You can text SHOUT to 85258.

The safety cards and the support card are there to help you reach people. They may not appear every time they could help, and they may sometimes appear when they are not needed.

Counts, insights, questionnaires and reports

Clear days, units, money and time are worked out from what you enter and are estimates. Insights show patterns in what you have recorded; a pattern is not proof that one thing causes another. The AUDIT, AUDIT-C and Severity of Dependence Scale are screening questionnaires, not a diagnosis. Reports are summaries of what you entered, to support a conversation with a professional, not clinical records.

Reminders and routines

Reminders are gentle and may arrive a few minutes late, or not at all if Android holds them back. Do not rely on Steady for anything time-critical, such as taking prescribed medication.
