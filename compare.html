What Steady does not do

Steady is self-help, not treatment, and it has no coaches, counsellors or community. It does not prescribe or supervise a detox. It is not on Google Play, so you install it from this site and install new versions over the old one.

Where the others do more

I Am Sober has a large community and covers many kinds of habit. Reframe offers coaching and a long daily course. Try Dry is run by the charity behind Dry January and offers a coaching email programme. All three are on Google Play and update themselves.

If you are in crisis

No app is a substitute for help. In the UK call 999 in an emergency, or Samaritans free on 116 123, any time. FRANK is on 0300 123 6600, any time.

Apps change. If a row is no longer right, open an issue on GitHub and it will be corrected.
