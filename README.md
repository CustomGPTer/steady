# Steady

Steady is a free Android app for stopping or cutting down on alcohol, drugs or both: clear-day counts that never reset to zero, Urge now in one tap, CBT and motivational tools, 40 short lessons, ten five-day programmes, a drink diary in UK units and a report for your GP or keyworker. Encrypted on your phone, no adverts, no account.
